# tau-watcher

Notes what the person likely missed. Every 6th step of a run, a side
request reads the conversation and answers `learn: none`, or offers one
short note, drawn under the step and, while new, above the composer. Off
until switched on in its settings pane. It is a Luau plugin tau comes
with (ADR 0050): its code is under `src/`. Reference:
`docs/reference/watcher.md`.
